## TechNova Inc. — AI Usage Policy
**Version 1.0 | Effective: January 2026 | Owner: Legal & IT Compliance**

---

## Section 1 — Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the use of artificial intelligence tools, large language models (LLMs),
and AI-assisted software by all TechNova employees, contractors, and third-party vendors
operating on behalf of TechNova. It applies to all departments and all work — internal
operations, client-facing deliverables, and personal use on company devices.

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## Section 2 — Approved AI Tools

The following AI tools are approved for use without prior manager approval:

| Tool | Approved uses | Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub Copilot | Code generation, code review, documentation | Engineering team only. No client data in prompts. |
| Claude (Anthropic) | Writing, summarization, analysis, research | All teams. No confidential data without encryption. |
| Grammarly | Writing assistance, grammar | All teams. No restricted documents. |
| Otter.ai | Meeting transcription | Internal meetings only. No client calls without consent. |

All other AI tools require written approval from the employee's direct manager AND
a review by the IT Security team before use. Unapproved tools may not be used
even on a trial basis.

---

## Section 3 — Prohibited Uses

The following uses of AI tools are strictly prohibited regardless of tool approval status:

1. Inputting personally identifiable information (PII) of customers, employees,
   or third parties into any AI tool not certified for PII handling.
2. Using AI to generate content that will be published externally without human review
   and explicit sign-off from a manager.
3. Using AI tools to make final decisions on hiring, firing, performance reviews,
   or compensation adjustments. AI may assist with drafting, not deciding.
4. Sharing unpublished financial data, acquisition targets, or M&A activity
   with any AI tool, approved or not.
5. Using AI to attempt to bypass security controls, access controls, or audit systems.
6. Using personal AI subscriptions (not company-provisioned) for company work
   involving any data classified as Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

---

## Section 4 — Data Classification and AI Use

TechNova classifies data into four levels. AI tool permissions vary by level:

| Classification | Definition | AI tool use allowed? |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Information already publicly available | Yes, any approved tool |
| Internal | Company information not for external sharing | Yes, approved tools only |
| Confidential | Sensitive business or client data | Only tools with signed DPA. Requires manager approval per use. |
| Restricted | PII, financial records, legal documents | No AI tools permitted without CISO written approval |

When in doubt about a document's classification, treat it as Confidential.
Employees are personally responsible for correctly classifying data before use.

---

## Section 5 — Contractor and Vendor Rules

Contractors working on TechNova projects must follow the same AI usage rules as
full-time employees with the following additional restrictions:

- Contractors may not use AI tools on TechNova client data under any circumstances
  without a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA) between TechNova, the contractor,
  and the AI vendor.
- Contractors must disclose all AI tools they use on TechNova work in their monthly
  activity report.
- Verbal approval from a TechNova manager does not constitute policy authorization
  for contractors. Written confirmation is required for every exception.
- Contractors who violate this policy may have their contract terminated immediately
  without notice period.

---

## Section 6 — Escalation and Exceptions

Employees who believe a policy exception is warranted must follow this process:

1. Submit an exception request to their direct manager in writing (email is acceptable).
2. Manager reviews and either rejects or forwards to IT Security within 3 business days.
3. IT Security reviews and either approves or escalates to the CISO within 5 business days.
4. CISO provides final written approval or denial.
5. Approved exceptions are logged in the IT Security exception registry and expire after 90 days.

Exceptions are not retroactive. Using a tool before an exception is approved is a policy violation
even if the exception is later granted.

Urgency is not grounds for bypassing the exception process. If an urgent situation arises,
contact your manager immediately — do not proceed with an unapproved tool.

---

## Section 7 — AI-Generated Content and Disclosure

Employees must disclose AI assistance in the following situations:

- Any client-facing document, report, or communication where more than 30% of the
  content was generated or substantially edited by an AI tool.
- Any internal document submitted as original research or analysis.
- Code submitted for production deployment where AI generated more than 50% of the logic.

Disclosure format: Add a footer note reading "Drafted with AI assistance — reviewed and
approved by [Name], [Date]."

Employees are fully responsible for the accuracy of AI-generated content they submit or publish.
Claiming AI error as a defense for inaccurate deliverables is not acceptable.

---

## Section 8 — Monitoring and Enforcement

TechNova IT may monitor AI tool usage on company devices and company-provisioned accounts.
Employees have no expectation of privacy when using company AI tool subscriptions.

Policy violations are reported to the employee's manager and HR. Repeated violations
escalate to the Legal team. The IT Security team conducts quarterly audits of AI tool
usage logs and exception registries.

Employees are encouraged to report suspected violations by colleagues to the IT Security
team anonymously via the internal ethics hotline.
